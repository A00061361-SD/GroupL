Consumer Technology Evolution – Social Media

Introduction and historical development.

The term "social media" was first used to describe the evolution of Web 2.0 applications that are open and social in nature. 
It has transformed how people communicate over the past three decades, impacting the lives of millions of people worldwide. 
Social media are online social networking sites that allow individuals, companies, organizations, and governments to communicate across the world, 
create and share content at any time and to spread and publish information such as medical studies, political issues and news seamlessly.
There are many different social media platforms that exist today, such as Facebook, Instagram, Snapchat, Reddit, WhatsApp, X, and online blogs.
