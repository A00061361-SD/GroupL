Consumer Technology Evolution – Social Media

Introduction and historical development.

The term "social media" was first used to describe the evolution of Web 2.0 applications that are open and social in nature. 
It has transformed how people communicate over the past three decades, impacting the lives of millions of people worldwide. 
Social media are online social networking sites that allow individuals, companies, organizations, and governments to communicate across the world, 
create and share content at any time and to spread and publish information such as medical studies, political issues and news seamlessly.
There are many different social media platforms that exist today, such as Facebook, Instagram, Snapchat, Reddit, WhatsApp, X, and online blogs. 

This group project gives a brief overview of Evolution of social media platforms,
the development of search platforms, the transformation of streaming platforms, the rise of ecommerce, evolution of digital photography, and the development of instant messaging. 
Social media sites such as MySpace started to allow people to create their own profiles and interact with friends whilst showing off what they were interested in. 
Facebook became a huge hit helping to increase the popularity of social media by allowing people to interact and share updates with each other. 
Social media has evolved over time with sites such as Instagram and TikTok allowing people to share photos and videos in new ways. 
This has turned social media from simple networking sites into interactive, entertaining and advertising mediums.
